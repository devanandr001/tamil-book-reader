Privacy Policy

Oli Tamil Book Reader · Last updated 12 October 2026

Oli Tamil Book Reader is an offline reading app. It does not require an account and does not send your reading activity, bookmarks, highlights, text corrections, or imported PDFs to a server.

Information stored on your device

The app stores bookmarks, saved highlights, and corrected page text in local app storage so they remain available on that device. The supplied book PDF is included with the app. If you choose another PDF, it is read locally by the app. These items are not uploaded by this version. The web-hosting provider may receive ordinary connection data needed to serve the website and PDF.

Copy feature

If you use Copy, the text you choose to copy is placed on your device's clipboard so you can paste it into another app.

Analytics and advertising

This version does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data deletion

You can clear this app's stored data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored bookmarks, highlights, and corrections. Uninstalling also removes app files.

Children

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Policy changes and contact

If the app later adds cloud sync, analytics, accounts, or other data collection, this policy and the app's Play Console Data safety answers must be updated before release. Replace this section with a monitored contact email before publishing.
